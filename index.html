Gabriel Karger

I am the Rappaport Fellow at Harvard Law School, where I research questions in torts and anti-discrimination/equality law. I'm especially interested in how courts do and should understand causation in both areas.

I am completing my PhD in Politics at Princeton University; I received my JD from Columbia Law School and my AB from Harvard College.
