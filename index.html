Gabriel Karger

I am a legal philosopher completing my PhD in Politics at Princeton University. I will soon begin as the Rappaport Fellow at Harvard Law School. My research mostly centers around questions in torts and anti-discrimination/equality law; I'm especially interested in the theory of causation in both areas.

I received my JD from Columbia Law School and my AB from Harvard College.
